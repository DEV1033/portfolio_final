Hi, I’m Dev, a self-taught product designer who found design through frontend development in the 2nd year of college.

I’m currently designing at Easycomm. Before that I was a Product Designer at Goodspace AI and a UI/UX Designer at GeeksforGeeks.

I’m a 2× design hackathon winner, and I love talking about Figma, motion design, micro-interactions and design systems.

How it started

A moment of curiosity

It was in the 2nd year of college. I was doing frontend development when suddenly a few questions struck me — “How are these interfaces made?” “Is there any way to do this?” That’s when I researched it and came to know about UI/UX design.

From 2nd year till now, my journey has been full of learning, gaining exposure to AI methodologies in the field, developing interfaces and so on. Tbh, it’s very fun!
